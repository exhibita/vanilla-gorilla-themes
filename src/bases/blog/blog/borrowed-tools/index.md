I do not own a drill. My neighbour does, and I have borrowed it nine times in four years.

Each time I return it, we talk for twenty minutes. I know about her allotment, her knee, and her opinion of the council. I would know none of this if I had bought my own drill.

## The economy of asking

A borrowed tool comes with advice. The person who owns it has already made the mistakes. "Start slow or the bit will wander," she said the first time, and it did not wander.

Buy the tools you use each week. Borrow the rest, and return them clean.

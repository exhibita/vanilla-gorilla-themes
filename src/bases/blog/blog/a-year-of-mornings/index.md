On 1 August last year I took a photograph of the corner outside my flat at seven in the morning. I took another the next day, and the day after that.

There are now 365 of them. Looking at them in a row is like watching a slow film.

## What changes

The light, of course. In December the photograph is almost black. In June the shadows are already short.

The smaller changes surprised me more. A bicycle was locked to the same railing for five months, and then it was gone. The bakery changed its awning. A tree I had not noticed was cut down in March.

## What stays

The man with the grey dog passes at 7.02 on most days. I have never spoken to him. He is in ninety-four of the photographs.

Fresh colour fades for three reasons: hot water, strong shampoo and sun. You control all three.

![A lock of freshly coloured hair](./images/work-3.svg)

## Wait before the first wash

Leave your hair for 48 hours after a colour visit. The cuticle needs that time to close.

## Turn the water down

Hot water opens the cuticle and lets colour out. Wash with warm water and rinse with cool water.

> If the water is comfortable for your face, it is right for your hair.

## Change what is in the shower

- Use a sulphate-free shampoo.
- Wash two or three times a week, not daily.
- Use a mask once a week in place of conditioner.

## Protect it from heat and sun

Use a heat protector each time you use a dryer or an iron. Wear a hat on the beach.

If your colour looks flat before your next full visit, a 45-minute gloss brings it back.

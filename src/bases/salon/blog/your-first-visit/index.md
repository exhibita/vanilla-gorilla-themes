A first visit takes a little longer than the ones after it. This is what to expect.

1. We talk for ten minutes before anything else. Bring photos.
2. You get an exact price before any work starts.
3. We wash, cut or colour, and show you how to style the result.
4. Before you leave, we tell you when to come back and why.

You can ask for a quiet appointment when you book. Many people do.

"Low maintenance" means different things to different people. These three questions make it specific.

## How often do you want to come in?

Tell us the real number. If the answer is twice a year, we choose a colour that grows out softly and a cut that keeps its shape.

## How long do you spend on your hair each morning?

Five minutes and thirty minutes need different haircuts. Neither answer is wrong.

## What do you never want to do?

Some people will never use a round brush. Say so. We plan the cut around how you live.

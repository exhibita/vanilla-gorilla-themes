The fast train takes two hours and ten minutes. The slow one takes six hours and stops at stations I had never heard of.

I took the slow one by accident. I would do it again on purpose.

## Time you cannot spend

With no signal and no table, there is very little to do. You look out of the window. After an hour, you start to see things: a heron in a drainage ditch, a football pitch with one goal, washing lines that all face the same way.

## The practical part

If you want to try it, here is what worked for me:

- Take food. The trolley does not exist.
- Sit on the side away from the sun.
- Bring one book, not three.

I arrived tired and late, and I remember all of it.
